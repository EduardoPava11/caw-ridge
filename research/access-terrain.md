## 1. What Caw Ridge is
- The name is NOT official: "Caw Ridge" is not in the CGNDB; only Caw Creek is. https://geogratis.gc.ca/services/geoname/en/geonames.json?q=Caw%20Ridge
- Formerly called Copton Ridge; AWA writes "Caw (Copton) Ridge". https://www.albertawilderness.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/20100600_ar_wla_recall_of_wild_doug_macfarlane.pdf
- The official CGNDB "Copton Ridge" point is about 11 km north of the waypoint.
- Goat study area centre: 54 03 N, 119 23 W. (snippet) https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2435.13514
- Alpine areas used by goats range 1,750 to 2,170 m. https://www.albertawilderness.ca/wp-content/uploads/20100400_ar_wla_goats_cote.pdf
- Treeline about 1,900 m (snippet from the Caw Ridge literature).
- Extent: about 28 km2 study area (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405896/); AWA gives about 21 km2 (https://www.albertawilderness.ca/issues/wildlands/areas-of-concern/kakwa/).
- Terrain: "a complex of rolling hills with short cliff faces and rockslides" (AWA Kakwa page); subalpine forest and alpine tundra, "a few rocky cliffs", surrounded by boreal forest (PMC6405896).
- A foothills outlier, separated from the main range by 10 to 30 km of coniferous forest, about 30 km northwest of Grande Cache (Cote 2010 PDF).
- Trailforks: 1,290 m at the trail bottom, 2,105 m at the top. (snippet) https://www.trailforks.com/trails/caw-ridge/
- Geology: Smoky River Coalfield, Lower Cretaceous non-marine clastics with seven coal seams (1.5 to 7 m), folded and thrust faulted (describes No. 8 Mine). https://miningdataonline.com/property/691/No.-8-(CST-Coal)-Mine.aspx
- Luscar Group = Cadomin, Gladstone, Moosebar, Gates formations. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luscar_Group
- 1974 to 1975: 150 test holes drilled "across the brow of Caw Ridge", about 20 years of surface mineable coal found (MacFarlane PDF).

## 2. Road access
- Hinton to Grande Cache about 140 km; Grande Prairie about 193 km; Hwy 40 paved. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Highway_40
- Edmonton about 431 to 435 km, about 4 h 42 min. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande_Cache
- STANDARD ROUTE (MD of Greenview): Beaverdam Road leaves Hwy 40 8.1 km north of Grande Cache and crosses coal mine property and the main mine road: "PLEASE MAKE SURE YOU STAY ON BEAVERDAM ROAD". 30 km along Beaverdam Road to the Caw Ridge turnoff on the left; the old road then climbs 8.1 km to the top. https://mdgreenview.ab.ca/tourism/quadding-and-snowmobiling/
- A forum post puts the turnoff at about the km 28 marker. https://www.snowandmud.com/threads/quading-in-grande-cache.80883/
- Conditions (forum, 2013 to 2023): Beaverdam Road "pretty rough" in a pickup, fine on an ATV; small trailers only. Sheep Creek Road is an active, better maintained oilfield road, gated at km 13 to highway vehicles. https://www.snowandmud.com/threads/caw-ridge-questions.129878/
- Older thread: sign saying no OHV use on Sheep Creek Road; gate with security shack at the Copton airstrip road. https://www.snowandmud.com/threads/kakwa-to-grande-cache.16579/
- The two roads connect via a creek crossing at the end of Beaverdam Road; "sketchy log bridge" and a Bailey bridge over Sheep Creek.
- The climb is an old mining exploration road, eroded, long cobble sections. A loop option descends south past the open pit to Beaverdam Road about 8 km east of the trailhead. (Trailforks snippet)
- Staging: mine flats by the river off Hwy 40; Gustavs Flats staging area; a staging area at the Caw Ridge turnoff.
- Radio: no published channel found. One forum post says radios on lease roads are required of commercial vehicles only. https://www.canadiangunnutz.com/forum/threads/hunting-in-north-west-alberta-wmu-440-446.2394823/
- Canfor road info line 780-538-7771. https://www.canfor.com/resources/grande-prairie-road-status
- Two Lakes Road is reached from Grande Prairie via Hwy 666, not from Grande Cache.
- Hwy 40 closed near Grande Prairie by a tanker fire 2026-09-15, cleared 2026-09-16. https://everythinggp.com/2026/09/15/highway-40-closed-due-to-tanker-truck-fire/

## 3. Trails and routes
- Trailforks "Caw Ridge": 10 km one way climb, 902 m gain, 5.8% average grade; ridge lines can be explored west and south from the top; stay on trails (ground nesting birds). (snippet)
- AllTrails "Caw Ridge OHV Route": 44.6 km out and back, 17 reviews. (snippet) https://www.alltrails.com/trail/canada/alberta/caw-ridge-ohv-route
- "just shy of 50 kms from the highway to Caw Ridge" (forum). Plotaroute 49 km: https://www.plotaroute.com/route/1177128
- Roads run most of the ridgeline. https://riderswestmag.com/alberta/article/4_grande_cache_locations_worth_traversing
- Too much snow to reach the top in late May 2008 and June 2013.
- OHV traffic rose from 30 to 40 people per summer (1994 to 1995) to more than 400 per summer by about 2009. At 35 km/h there is a greater than 50% chance goats stay alert up to 10 minutes or move at least 100 m (Cote 2010 PDF).

## 4. Industrial activity
- McIntyre Porcupine Mines permit on Caw Ridge 1969; Smoky River Coal 1987, receivership March 2000; Grande Cache Coal leases September 2000. (AWA Kakwa page)
- B2 pit extension approved October 1999; No. 12 Mine South B2 Phase 1 approved 2004 to 2005. B-2 pit lies "on the eastern slope of Caw Ridge and at the headwaters of Beaverdam Creek", about 20 km NW of Grande Cache.
- 1976: Caw ended up Category 4, open to mining (MacFarlane PDF).
- CST Canada Coal bought assets 2018, halted May 2020, resumed October 2021. https://www.gem.wiki/Grande_Cache_Coal_Mine
- CST operates No. 8 surface mine, 17 to 20 km north of Grande Cache, 4,900 ha permit. https://cstcoal.ca/about-us/
- CST applied for No. 12 South A Underground Mine; AWA statement of concern 2026-05-20. https://www.albertawilderness.ca/statement-of-concern-on-cst-canada-coal-ltd-s-proposed-no-12-south-a-underground-mine-development/
- Horn Ridge Resources proposed coal exploration Twp 58, Rges 9 and 10 W6M, immediately south of Caw Ridge (2020). https://albertawilderness.ca/awa-statement-of-concern-horn-ridge-coal-exploration-program/
- Coal moratorium ended 2025-01-15. https://www.alberta.ca/coal-industry-modernization-initiative
- Caw Ridge is unprotected; AWA wants it designated. Kakwa Wildland Provincial Park 649.3 km2 (1996).

## 5. Wildlife
- Goat study: preliminary 1988, full seasons from June 1989, Universite Laval / Sherbrooke; Steeve Cote and Sandra Hamel. https://labofestabianchet.weebly.com/research.html
- September 2009: 134 goats, 120 marked; 427 marked since 1988; the only long term study of the species; largest Alberta population outside the national parks (Cote 2010).
- Goats on Caw Ridge NOT HUNTED SINCE 1969; provincial goat hunting closed 1988 to 2000, reopened 2001.
- Adult female survival 89%, male 83%, kid 63%; first reproduction almost 5 years; males about 100 kg, females 75 kg.
- Population ranged 76 to 160+, declining 2004 to 2017 (snippets). https://www.rmoutlook.com/local-news/study-reveals-life-of-mountain-goats-1561194
- Predators: grizzly, wolf, cougar mainly.
- Helicopters: 85% of flights within 500 m moved goats more than 100 m; 2 km buffer recommended.
- Goat rut mid to late November.
- Bighorn sheep: about 250, mostly on the eastern part of the ridge and adjacent lands (Cote 2010).
- Caribou: major migration route for the Redrock-Prairie Creek herd; about 96 (RPC) and 28 (Narraway) in 2017 (AWA); minimum count 138 both ranges 2023 (snippet).
- Grizzly: BMA 2 (Grande Cache) about 18 per 1,000 km2 (2008), highest in the province. https://friresearch.ca/news/estimated-population-status-grizzly-bears-alberta/
- Fresh grizzly tracks regularly reported on the access, especially near the Copton fire tower (forum).
- Rut timing (general): elk Sept 1 to Oct 15; moose late Sept to mid Oct, peak early Oct; mule deer late Oct to early Dec, peak mid Nov; bighorn late Nov to mid Dec.

## 7. Safety
- Alberta bears and hunters guidance: bear spray + noisemaker on body; noise while field dressing; gut pile at least 200 m from carcass; hang meat at least 3.5 m up and 1.5 m from trunk; store at least 100 m from camp; approach carcass from upwind with binoculars; bear on it: leave, call 310-0000. https://www.alberta.ca/bears-and-hunters
- Hospital: Grande Cache Community Health Complex, 10200 Shand Avenue, 24 hour emergency, 780-827-7627 / 780-827-7625. https://www.albertahealthservices.ca/findhealth/Service.aspx?id=6915&serviceAtFacilityID=1075604
- STARS nearest base: Grande Prairie Airport.
- Grande Cache SAR deploys under police: call 911. https://www.gcsar.ca/
- Report A Poacher 1-800-642-3800; wildfire 310-FIRE; Fish and Wildlife 310-0000.
- Cell: town only; assume none on the ridge.

## 9. History
- Grande Cache named for a large fur cache left by Ignace Giasson, 1820. https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/grande-cache
- Aseniwuche Winewak ("Rocky Mountain people") moved from Jasper early 1900s.
- Dinosaur tracksite: Provincial Historic Resource 2006, more than 10,000 footprints on footwalls tilted 40 to 60 degrees, in the former Smoky River mine. https://hermis.alberta.ca/ARHP/Details.aspx?DeptID=1&ObjectID=4665-1333
