- Elk 6 point: a main beam with at least five tines, each at least 7.6 cm (3 in).
- Sheep class 1 is the 4/5 curl rule: a line from the front of the horn base to the tip extends beyond the front edge of the eye. No general or non-resident class 1 season in 446. The general class 1 season in 444 applies only south of Beaverdam Road.
- Goat: Schedule 15 Table 1D lists goat only for WMUs 440, 442, 444 (Sep 10 to Oct 31) and 400, 402, all special licence.
- Black bear: NO baiting in 446. Supplemental (second bear) licence not valid in 446. Dogs permitted Sept and Oct. Unlawful to take a cub under one year or a female with such a cub.
- Cougar: Cougar Management Area 27 (WMUs 355, 445, 446). Winter: call 1-800-661-3729 each day before hunting. Report harvest within 24 hours.
- Wolf: resident needs no licence; non-resident needs a wildlife certificate. Season from opening of any big game season in the WMU to May 31, 2027 (June 15 where bear seasons run to June 15; unclear for 446).
- Sharp-tailed grouse: no season listed for 446.

## 3. Caw Ridge specific
- NO Caw Ridge sanctuary or closure in law (Wildlife Regulation Schedules 11, 12 have no entry).
- Goat study since June 1989; "Goats on Caw Ridge have not been hunted since 1969." https://www.albertawilderness.ca/wp-content/uploads/20100400_ar_wla_goats_cote.pdf ; https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2656.13002
- Marked animals: anyone who kills or finds an animal with a tracking device must report and return it. Marked animals may carry a tag warning not to eat the meat before contacting Fish and Wildlife (guide p. 64).
- No Public Land Use Zone at the point (GIS; none of the 18 listed zones is near Grande Cache). https://www.alberta.ca/public-land-use-zones
- OHV WEAPONS RULE (WMUs 400 to 446): unlawful to carry a weapon on an OHV from 1 hour before sunrise to the following noon during an open big game season. Applies to bird hunters too. Exception: direct travel to an isolated campsite with weapons and ammunition out of view in separate locked containers.
- Other 400 series rules: no helicopter transport of big game hunters or game; no hunting big game within 6 hours of leaving an aircraft; no pack goats or domestic sheep; pack dogs only if leashed or within 50 m.
- PENDING: Upper Smoky Sub-regional Plan (in force November 2025) names "Caw Ridge, Sulphur Ridge and Coal/Horn/Torrens Ridge (Conservation Area A)" for designation under the Provincial Parks Act. Hunting "will continue in accordance with existing provincial laws"; motorized recreation "will be permitted in areas identified on a map published by the Department." Also calls for a PLUZ near Grande Cache. https://open.alberta.ca/publications/upper-smoky-sub-regional-plan
- GIS still shows no protected area at the point, so designation appears not yet done.

## 4. Caribou
- No caribou season. Threatened (Wildlife Regulation Schedule 6). Guide p. 42.
- Closed since 1981 (secondary: https://naturealberta.ca/four-decades-of-neglect/).
- The point is inside the Redrock-Prairie Creek range (Caribou Range Zone A). A La Peche about 36 km SE; Narraway about 48 km NW. https://geospatial.alberta.ca/titan/rest/services/biota/wildlife_sensitivity_mammals_10tm_nad83_aep/MapServer
- No caribou based recreational closure found.

## 6. Land status
- Provincial Crown land, Green Area, MD of Greenview No. 16, Treaty 8, Forest Protection Area. Not in any park. https://geospatial.alberta.ca/mimas/rest/services/boundaries/land_green_white_area_public/MapServer ; https://geospatial.alberta.ca/titan/rest/services/boundary/parks_protected_areas_alberta/MapServer
- Willmore Wilderness Park: nearest boundary about 8 km southwest. Hunting permitted; no OHV or snowmobile. Hinton Parks office 780-865-8395. https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/central/willmore/
- Sulphur Gates staging: firearm discharge permit; no hunting within 200 m of facilities or the access road.
- Kakwa Wildland Provincial Park: about 12 km west-southwest. Hunting permitted; OHVs on designated trails only. 780-538-5350. https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/northwest/kakwa-wpp/
- Coal lease: the point is inside active Crown coal lease 1321020119, CST Canada Coal Limited 100%, expiring 2036-02-17. Mineral tenure, not a surface closure. https://geospatial.alberta.ca/titan/rest/services/energy/mineral_agreement/MapServer
- CST 2026 application, No. 12 South A underground mine. https://www.aer.ca/applications-and-notices/application-status-and-notices/notices/applications-1961168-020-00155804
- Beaverdam Road: see access notes. https://mdgreenview.ab.ca/tourism/quadding-and-snowmobiling/
- Guide's only coal lease big game closures are Gregg River and Cardinal River (WMU 438). Nothing for Grande Cache. General law: no hunting or discharge on occupied land without consent; none within 183 m of an occupied building.

## 7. Licensing and general rules (2026 guide)
- WiN ($10 activation) + wildlife certificate ($35; $12 resident youth or senior; $80 non-resident alien) + species licence + paper tags (cannot be electronic).
- Bowhunting permit: resident $10. Crossbows cannot be used in archery only seasons.
- Resident prices before GST: deer $50, elk $50, moose archery $50, antlered moose special $65, class 1 sheep special $95, black bear $30, cougar $30, game bird $20.
- Non-residents hunting big game, wolf or coyote must be with a designated guide (outfitter) or licensed hunter host. Non-resident Canadian: deer $193, antlered elk $275, black bear $110, cougar $165, game bird $77.
- Compulsory registration: male sheep over one year, and goat, within 14 days of season close or 30 days of the kill, whichever is first. Cougar within five business days. In person by appointment. Nearest designated sheep and goat offices: Grande Prairie and Edson.
- CWD: WMU 446 NOT on the 2026 mandatory deer head submission list.
- Hunter orange: not required.
- Legal hours: unlawful to hunt or discharge a firearm between one-half hour after sunset and one-half hour before sunrise.
- Roads: no discharge from, along or across a provincial highway or a road that is paved, oiled, graded or regularly maintained (exceptions: roads under active Public Lands Act disposition; shotgun bird hunting). No loaded firearm in or on a vehicle.
- Sunday hunting allowed in 446.
- Ammunition: no rimfire, no centrefire under .22 calibre, no non-expanding bullets for big game. Auto-loaders limited to five in the magazine.

## 9. Indigenous context
- Aseniwuche Winewak Nation: four cooperatives (Victor Lake, Susa Creek, Muskeg Seepee, Wanyandie Flats) and two enterprises (Grande Cache Lake/Kamisak, Joachim). Seven parcels, 4,150 acres, 1974 agreements, "along an 80 km stretch of Highway 40", communally held. https://www.aseniwuche.ca/awn-and-the-communities/
- Treat as private occupied land; permission required. Office 10003 99 Street, Grande Cache, 780-827-5510.
- Mountain Metis, 9711 99 Ave, Grande Cache. Point is in Metis Harvesting Area Region D. https://www.mountainmetis.com/ ; https://www.alberta.ca/metis-harvesting-rights
- Treaty and Metis harvesters may lawfully be hunting outside licensed seasons.
